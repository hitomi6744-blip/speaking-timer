3. Student instructions / အသုံးပြုပုံ

  1. Enter your Student ID or name.
  2. Choose your practice target.
  3. Press START PRACTICE.
  4. Open ChatGPT or Gemini and start a Japanese voice conversation.
  5. Return to this timer when finished. The elapsed timer uses timestamps, so it should account for time spent in another app.
  6. Press FINISH & MAKE REPORT, then copy the result and send it to your teacher.
